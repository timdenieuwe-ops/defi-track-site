Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

DeFi Track is a read-only portfolio-tracking app for supported DeFi liquidity positions. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and when information is sent to third-party data providers.

Information you provide

You may add public blockchain wallet addresses and optional local wallet names so DeFi Track can retrieve supported positions and display them in the app.

DeFi Track does not ask for, store, or transmit seed phrases, recovery phrases, private keys, wallet passwords or signing credentials.

Information stored on your device

DeFi Track stores app data locally on your device, including saved wallet addresses and names, preferences, cached portfolio snapshots, available history, position data, activity records and related app state. Some information is also shared locally with the app's widgets and paired Apple Watch through Apple's app-group and Watch connectivity mechanisms.

The app does not provide an app-owned cloud account or account-based sync service in the current release.

Information sent to third-party services

To provide portfolio, blockchain and price information, DeFi Track sends public blockchain information to external data providers. Depending on the feature and network, this can include public wallet addresses, token contract addresses, blockchain contract calls, transaction identifiers or related public on-chain identifiers.

The current app can access services including:

  • Public blockchain RPC providers for supported networks
  • Block explorers such as Blockscout and chain-specific explorers
  • Dexscreener for current token price information
  • DefiLlama for historical token price information
  • Frankfurter / ECB-derived exchange-rate data for USD/EUR conversion
  • Protocol-operated public endpoints when required for supported network access

These services are independent third parties. They may receive normal network information such as your IP address as part of an internet request and may process requests under their own privacy policies and terms. DeFi Track does not control their independent data handling.

Analytics and advertising

DeFi Track does not include an app-owned advertising SDK or analytics SDK in the current release. The app does not sell personal data.

Financial transactions

DeFi Track is read-only. It can prepare or simulate certain supported contract interactions for review and can open an official provider website, but the app itself does not sign or broadcast wallet transactions. Any wallet signing is performed outside DeFi Track by the user's chosen wallet or provider.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored by DeFi Track is primarily local to your device. Removing a tracked wallet removes it from the app's active wallet list, while some previously saved local history may remain to preserve app history or support safe upgrades. Removing the app from your device removes its normal app container according to iOS behavior, subject to any backups or platform-managed storage outside the app's control.

If you contact support through GitHub, the information you choose to post there is handled by GitHub under GitHub's own privacy terms. Do not post private keys, seed phrases or sensitive financial information in a public support issue.

Children

DeFi Track is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app is intended as an informational portfolio tool for users who independently manage supported DeFi positions.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, data providers or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact and support

For support or privacy questions, use the DeFi Track support page.
